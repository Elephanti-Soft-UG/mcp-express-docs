# Google Analytics Integration

The Google Analytics integration allows MCP Express to interact with your GA4 setup. It supports a wide range of reporting actions — from active users and traffic sources to real-time visitors and conversion analysis — all authenticated through a Google service account.

## Capabilities

- **Audience Reporting**: Retrieve active users, new users, and session data across custom date ranges.
- **Traffic & Acquisition Analysis**: Break down traffic by source, medium, and channel group to understand how users find your site.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Query live visitor counts, active screens, and device breakdowns without any date range constraints.
- **Event & Conversion Tracking**: Analyze top events, conversion counts, and user engagement across your GA4 property.
- **Demographic & Device Insights**: Segment your audience by country, city, language, device type, OS, and browser.
- **Date Range Comparison**: Compare two time periods side by side for sessions, active users, page views, and conversions.
- **Service Account Authentication**: Connect securely using a Google service account JSON key with read-only access to your GA4 property.
